German words you will meet

Mein ELSTER, the tax offices' portal, can show its menus in English. The tax forms and their help texts stay German. These are the words that decide how much comes back.

Lohnsteuerbescheinigung annual wage tax certificate
Your employer sends it to the tax office and gives you a copy early in the following year. Almost every number in an employee's return comes from it.
Werbungskosten work-related costs
What you pay to do or get your job: the way to work, a laptop, courses, applications. The tax office assumes 1,230 euros a year (1,200 for 2022) without asking. You gain only from what you can show beyond that.
Entfernungspauschale commuter allowance
For 2022 to 2025: 30 cents for each kilometre of the one-way distance on every day you go to work, 38 cents from the 21st kilometre. How you travel does not matter.
Progressionsvorbehalt progression clause
What you earned abroad before you arrived is not taxed in Germany, but it raises the rate on your German salary. It has to be declared, and leaving it out is a common mistake in the year you arrive.
Umzugskosten moving costs
A move for a job counts as a work cost, also when the move is to Germany: transport, travel, and rent paid twice for a while.
Doppelte Haushaltsführung second household for work
A flat near work while your main home stays elsewhere, also abroad, can count: rent up to 1,000 euros a month for a flat in Germany, and trips home.
Unterhalt support for family
Money you send to parents, a spouse or children abroad can be deducted up to a yearly limit that depends on the country they live in and on their own income. From 2025 only bank transfers count, not cash.
Kapitalerträge investment income
Brokers outside Germany, such as Interactive Brokers, do not withhold German tax. Gains, dividends and interest then belong in your return, and declared losses carry forward.

What it is, and what it is not

It is

  • Software for preparing your own income tax return, in English or German.
  • Built for employees and students, including people who moved to Germany during the year.
  • Private where it can be: the estimate on this page runs in your browser and sends nothing.

  • Tax advice. Nobody here reviews your individual case or acts for you at the tax office.
  • A service that sends your return. You file it yourself in Mein ELSTER, the tax offices' free portal.
  • Built for self-employed people, businesses or landlords.

Questions

Is it really all in English?

Yes. Questions, explanations and results are in English, and you can switch to German at any time. Official form words stay in German next to their English meaning, because that is what you will see in Mein ELSTER and in letters from the tax office.

Do I have to file a tax return?

Many employees do not have to, but may, and most who do get money back. Filing becomes mandatory in some cases, for example with income abroad in the same year, unemployment or parental benefit above 410 euros, the tax class combination III and V, or two employers at the same time.

How accurate is the estimate?

It uses the tax tariff of the year you choose and models what an employer withholds. It cannot know your exact health insurance rate or costs you have not entered, so it shows a range. The exact figure needs your wage tax certificate.

I arrived in the middle of the year. Why does that matter so much?

Your employer taxes each month as if you earned that salary for twelve months. If you worked only part of the year, your yearly income is lower and so is your tax. The return gives the difference back, unless income abroad pushes your rate up again.

What if the estimate shows no refund?

Then it says so. If filing is voluntary for you, you can simply not file. If you are obliged to file, for example because you had income abroad in the same year, the return is due either way, and it helps to know the amount early.

The tax office's app now files a return in one click. Why would I need this?

Since July 2026 the MeinELSTER+ app can prepare a simple return for 2025 from the data the tax office already holds. For a full year in one job with nothing to deduct, that may be all you need. It cannot know about costs nobody reported to it, such as your move, a long way to work, a second home or family you support abroad, unless you add them yourself. Income you had abroad before you arrived is not in its data either, although it has to be declared.

Which years can I still file?

If filing is voluntary for you, you have four years: 2022 until 31 December 2026, 2023 until the end of 2027, and so on.

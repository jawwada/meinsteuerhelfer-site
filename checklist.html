Everything that can lower your tax

The 55 questions Mein Steuerhelfer asks. For each one: what it does to your tax, and the rule behind it in plain words, next to the German word you will meet on the forms.

Amounts are for 2025 unless a year is named. This is general information about the rules for employees, not advice on your individual case.

Your year in Germany

Unbeschränkte Steuerpflicht goes in Hauptvordruck

Did you move to Germany, or leave it, during the year?

What it does to your tax
Usually a refund. Your employer taxed every month as if you earned that salary all year, but the yearly tax is worked out on what you actually earned in Germany.
The rule
From the day you live here you are taxed like any resident, on the yearly tariff with the full basic allowance of 12,096 euros. The months before you came or after you left are not taxed in Germany, but what you earned in them is asked for (see the next question).

Progressionsvorbehalt goes in Anlage WA-ESt

What did you earn abroad in the months of that year when you did not live in Germany?

What it does to your tax
It lowers the refund, sometimes to a payment. Above 410 euros it also makes the return mandatory.
The rule
This income stays tax-free in Germany, but your German income is taxed at the rate that both together would have. Give the amount in euros after the costs of earning it, and keep payslips or the foreign tax assessment as proof.

Zusammenveranlagung goes in Hauptvordruck

Are you married or in a registered partnership, and where does your partner live?

What it does to your tax
Filing together taxes each of you on half of the joint income. The more your incomes differ, the more it saves.
The rule
You can file together when both of you live in Germany. A partner living in another EU or EEA country can be included on request if nearly all of your joint income is taxed in Germany. For a partner living elsewhere, filing together is not possible; money you send may count as support instead.

Steuerklasse goes in Lohnsteuerbescheinigung

Which tax class did you have, and did it change during the year?

What it does to your tax
The class only decides how much is withheld each month. The return corrects it to the real yearly tax, upwards or downwards.
The rule
Classes III and V, or IV with a factor, make the return mandatory. A class that was wrong for part of the year, for example class I after a wedding, is put right by the return.

Kirchensteuer goes in Anlage Sonderausgaben

Are you registered as a member of a church that collects church tax?

What it does to your tax
You pay 8 or 9 percent on top of your income tax, and what you paid is deductible in full.
The rule
What counts is the religion recorded when you registered your address. Leaving the church ends the tax, in most states from the following month. Church tax withheld from your pay is taken from the wage tax certificate automatically.

Lohnersatzleistungen goes in Anlage N

Did you receive unemployment benefit, parental allowance, sick pay from the health insurer or short-time work pay?

What it does to your tax
These payments are tax-free but raise the rate on your other income, so they can lead to a payment. Above 410 euros in a year they make the return mandatory.
The rule
The paying office reports the amounts to the tax office. They are added to your income only to find the tax rate (Progressionsvorbehalt); that rate is then applied to your taxable income alone.

Lohnsteuerabzug goes in Anlage N

Did you have months without a job, change employer, or have two jobs at once?

What it does to your tax
Months without pay or a change during the year usually mean a refund. Two jobs at the same time, with tax class VI on the second, make the return mandatory and can go either way.
The rule
Each employer withholds as if their pay were your whole year. The return adds all wage tax certificates together and works out the tax once for the year.

Fünftelregelung goes in Anlage N

Did you receive a severance payment or pay for several years in one go?

What it does to your tax
The tax on it can be lower, because it is worked out as if the payment were spread over five years.
The rule
The payment must be concentrated in one year. Since 2025 employers no longer apply this rule on the payslip, so you only get it through the tax return.

What your job cost you

Werbungskosten goes in Anlage N

What did you pay yourself in order to do, keep or get your job?

What it does to your tax
Every euro above the flat amount lowers your taxable income. At a tax rate of 30 percent, 1,000 euros of extra costs bring back about 300 euros.
The rule
The tax office deducts 1,230 euros a year (1,200 for 2022) for every employee without asking. That is the default. Your own costs only count once their total is higher: the way to work, home-office days, equipment, phone, training, applications, a move, a second home, work trips, union fees. The questions below go through them one by one.

Entfernungspauschale goes in Anlage N

How far is it from your home to your workplace, and on how many days did you go there?

What it does to your tax
Often the largest work cost. A long way to work can pass the flat amount on its own.
The rule
30 cents for each kilometre of the one-way distance on every day you went to the workplace, 38 cents from kilometre 21. It counts however you travel, also on foot or by bike. Without your own car the yearly amount is capped at 4,500 euros, unless your real ticket costs were higher.

Tagespauschale goes in Anlage N

On how many days did you work from home instead of going to the workplace?

What it does to your tax
Up to 1,260 euros a year of extra work costs, without any receipts.
The rule
6 euros for each day you worked mainly at home and did not go to the workplace, for at most 210 days. You need no separate room. If a room at home is the centre of all your work, you can claim its real costs or a flat 1,260 euros a year instead.

Arbeitsmittel goes in Anlage N

Did you buy things you need for work: a laptop, monitor, phone, desk, chair, software, books, tools?

What it does to your tax
The work share of the price counts as a work cost, for most items in full in the year you bought them.
The rule
Items up to 800 euros before VAT (952 euros with VAT) are deducted at once. Dearer items are spread over their useful life, except computers and software, which may be deducted in the year of purchase. If you also use the item privately, only the work share counts. Keep the invoices.

Telekommunikation goes in Anlage N

Do you use your own phone or internet connection for work?

What it does to your tax
Up to 240 euros a year without proving a single call.
The rule
Without detailed proof the tax office accepts 20 percent of your phone and internet bills, at most 20 euros a month. With itemised bills for three months you can claim your real work share.

Fortbildungskosten goes in Anlage N

Did you pay for courses, certificates, exams, conferences, books or a language course you need for your job?

What it does to your tax
It counts in full, including travel to the course at 30 cents for each kilometre driven.
The rule
The training must relate to your current job or to one you are aiming for. German lessons count when your job requires the language; a general course for everyday life does not. Subtract anything your employer paid back.

Bewerbungskosten goes in Anlage N

Did you apply for jobs, also for ones you did not get?

What it does to your tax
All costs of applying count: photos, translations and certified copies of certificates, postage, travel to interviews.
The rule
Unsuccessful applications count too. Instead of single receipts, tax offices commonly accept about 2.50 euros for each online application and 8.50 euros for each one sent by post. Travel to interviews counts at 30 cents for each kilometre driven, unless the company paid.

Anerkennung ausländischer Abschlüsse goes in Anlage N

Did you pay to have a foreign degree or professional qualification recognised in Germany?

What it does to your tax
Fees, translations and certified copies count as work costs.
The rule
The costs must serve working in your profession here: the recognition procedure, the statement of comparability for a university degree, or the adaptation courses and exams it requires.

Umzugskosten goes in Anlage N

Did you move home because of your job, within Germany or to Germany?

What it does to your tax
Often more than a thousand euros of work costs in the year of the move.
The rule
The move must be for the job: a new job, a transfer, or a daily way to work that becomes at least an hour shorter. Transport, travel to the new home, rent paid twice for a time and an estate agent for a rented flat count with receipts. On top there is a flat amount for everything else: 964 euros for a move finished from March 2024 (886 euros before), plus an amount for each further person moving with you. Subtract what your employer paid.

Doppelte Haushaltsführung goes in Anlage N

Do you keep a second home near work while your main home is somewhere else, also abroad?

What it does to your tax
One of the largest deductions there is: rent, trips home and, at the start, meals.
The rule
Your main home must stay the centre of your life, and you must share in its running costs. Then these count: rent and running costs of the second home in Germany up to 1,000 euros a month, one trip home a week at the commuter rates, meal allowances of 14 or 28 euros a day for the first three months, and necessary furnishings.

Reisekosten goes in Anlage N

Did you travel for work, to customers, other sites or training, without getting everything paid back?

What it does to your tax
The part your employer did not reimburse counts as a work cost.
The rule
With your own car 30 cents for each kilometre driven, otherwise the real ticket price. For absences of more than eight hours 14 euros a day, for full days away 28 euros, at one place for at most three months. Hotel costs count as paid.

Berufskleidung goes in Anlage N

Did you pay for typical work clothing or for cleaning it?

What it does to your tax
Purchase and cleaning count as work costs.
The rule
Only clothing made for the job counts: protective gear, uniforms, lab or medical clothing. Ordinary clothes such as a business suit do not, even if you only wear them at work.

Berufsverbände goes in Anlage N

Are you a member of a union or a professional association?

What it does to your tax
The membership fees count in full as work costs.
The rule
The association must represent your profession: a trade union, an engineers' or doctors' association, or a chamber you have to belong to.

Kontoführungsgebühren goes in Anlage N

Is your salary paid into a bank account that costs you fees?

What it does to your tax
16 euros a year. Small, but accepted without proof.
The rule
Tax offices accept a flat 16 euros a year for the share of the account fees that your salary causes.

Berufliche Versicherungen goes in Anlage N

Do you pay for insurance that covers your work: professional liability, legal protection for employment disputes, accident insurance?

What it does to your tax
The work-related share counts as a work cost instead of disappearing in the capped insurance amount.
The rule
Professional liability counts in full. Of a combined policy only the work share counts: usually half of an accident insurance that covers work and leisure, and the employment part of a legal protection policy as stated by the insurer.

Family, here and abroad

Kindergeld und Kinderfreibetrag goes in Anlage Kind

Do you have children, living with you or abroad?

What it does to your tax
Child benefit of 255 euros a month, or the tax saving from the child allowance if that is worth more. Children also lower church tax and the solidarity surcharge.
The rule
The allowance is 9,600 euros per child for both parents together. The tax office compares it with the child benefit you were entitled to and applies the better one by itself. For a child living outside the EU and EEA there is usually no child benefit, and the allowance is reduced for countries with lower living costs. Children over 18 count while they are in education, up to their 25th birthday.

Kinderbetreuungskosten goes in Anlage Kind

Did you pay for a nursery, kindergarten, childminder, after-school care or a babysitter?

What it does to your tax
80 percent of the costs, up to 4,800 euros per child a year, lower your taxable income.
The rule
For children under 14 in your household. You need an invoice or contract and must pay by bank transfer; cash does not count. Meals, lessons and sports are not childcare. Until 2024 it was two thirds of the costs, up to 4,000 euros.

Entlastungsbetrag für Alleinerziehende goes in Anlage Kind

Do you live alone with your child, without another adult in the household?

What it does to your tax
4,260 euros of your income stay tax-free, plus 240 euros for each further child.
The rule
The child must be registered at your address and you must be entitled to child benefit for it. No other adult may live with you, apart from your own children in education. Tax class II already includes the basic amount each month.

Schulgeld goes in Anlage Kind

Does your child go to a private school that charges fees?

What it does to your tax
30 percent of the fees, up to 5,000 euros per child a year.
The rule
The school must lead to a recognised school-leaving or vocational qualification and be in the EU or EEA, or be a German school abroad. Boarding, care and meals do not count.

Ausbildungsfreibetrag goes in Anlage Kind

Is an adult child of yours in training or at university and living away from home?

What it does to your tax
1,200 euros a year on top of the child benefit or allowance (924 euros for 2022).
The rule
The child must be over 18, in education and living away from your household, and you must still be entitled to child benefit for them. The amount is given for the months in which all of this was true.

Unterhalt goes in Anlage Unterhalt

Do you send money to your parents, grandparents, your spouse or your children abroad?

What it does to your tax
Up to 12,096 euros per supported person a year lower your taxable income. For countries with lower living costs the limit is three quarters, half or a quarter of that. The person's own income above 624 euros a year is subtracted.
The rule
It counts for relatives the law obliges you to support: parents, grandparents, children, your spouse. They must be in need, with little income and savings under 15,500 euros, and people of working age are expected to work unless they cannot. You need a statement of need certified by their local authority (the two-language Unterhaltserklärung) and proof of payment. Since 2025 only bank transfers to the person's own account count; before that, cash handed over on a visit counted with proof. The limit runs from the month of your first payment, so pay early in the year.

Unterhalt bei Haushaltsaufnahme goes in Anlage Unterhalt

Did your parents or another close relative live with you in Germany for part of the year?

What it does to your tax
For the months they lived with you, food and housing count as support without receipts, up to the monthly share of 12,096 euros a year.
The rule
When a relative you are obliged to support lives in your household, the tax office assumes that you bear costs up to the limit. You need proof of the stay, such as tickets, entry stamps or the registration, and of the person's need. Their own income is subtracted as with money you send.

Unterhalt für Erwachsene ohne Kindergeld goes in Anlage Unterhalt

Do you support a student or another adult without income of their own, for example a child over 25 or the partner you live with?

What it does to your tax
The same limit of 12,096 euros a year applies, less the person's own income above 624 euros. Grants and scholarships count as their income.
The rule
A child counts here once nobody receives child benefit for them, typically after the 25th birthday. A partner you live with counts when public benefits are refused or cut because of your income. Brothers, sisters and other relatives do not count, however much you help them.

Verpflichtungserklärung goes in Anlage Unterhalt

Did you sign a formal commitment at the immigration office to pay for someone's stay in Germany?

What it does to your tax
On its own it brings no deduction. It matters in one situation only.
The rule
For parents, children and a spouse, support counts anyway because the law obliges you; the commitment adds nothing. For brothers, sisters and other relatives it does not make support deductible, as the Federal Fiscal Court ruled in 2021. The exception: for people admitted under the humanitarian programmes of § 23 of the Residence Act, support counts if you signed the commitment and bear all their living costs.

Realsplitting goes in Anlage U

Do you pay maintenance to a divorced or separated spouse?

What it does to your tax
Up to 13,805 euros a year can be deducted, if the recipient agrees to pay tax on it.
The rule
Both of you sign Anlage U. The recipient must live in Germany or, with proof that the payments are taxed there, in another EU or EEA country. Without consent the payments may count as support under the general limit instead.

Pflege-Pauschbetrag goes in Anlage Außergewöhnliche Belastungen

Do you care for a relative at home without being paid for it?

What it does to your tax
A flat 600, 1,100 or 1,800 euros a year, depending on the care level.
The rule
The person must have care level 2 or higher, and the care must take place in your home or theirs, in the EU or EEA. You must not be paid for it.

Insurance, pension, donations, studies

Kranken- und Pflegeversicherung goes in Anlage Vorsorgeaufwand

Did you pay health or long-term care insurance yourself: private cover, a foreign insurer, or contributions for your spouse and children?

What it does to your tax
Contributions for basic cover are deductible without a limit.
The rule
Statutory contributions come from your wage tax certificate automatically. Private and foreign policies count for the part that matches basic statutory cover; extras such as a single hospital room do not. Contributions you pay for your spouse, or for children you get child benefit for, count as your own.

Sonstige Vorsorgeaufwendungen goes in Anlage Vorsorgeaufwand

Do you pay for personal liability, accident, occupational disability, car liability or term life insurance?

What it does to your tax
Often nothing, because the limit is usually used up by health insurance. Enter them anyway: it costs nothing and helps when your health contributions are low.
The rule
These contributions count only up to 1,900 euros a year for employees, together with health and care contributions. Household contents, comprehensive car cover and legal protection for private matters never count.

Altersvorsorge goes in Anlage Vorsorgeaufwand, Anlage AV

Do you pay into a pension beyond the statutory one: a Riester or Rürup contract, or voluntary pension contributions?

What it does to your tax
Statutory and Rürup contributions lower your taxable income in full (94 percent for 2022). For Riester you get allowances or, if that is better, a deduction of up to 2,100 euros.
The rule
Your statutory pension contributions are taken from the wage tax certificate. A private contract must be certified in Germany to count; most foreign pension plans are not.

Spenden goes in Anlage Sonderausgaben

Did you donate to a charity, a religious community or a political party, or pay membership fees to a charitable association?

What it does to your tax
Donations lower your taxable income, up to 20 percent of your income. Donations to political parties reduce the tax directly by half of the amount, up to 825 euros.
The rule
The organisation must be recognised as charitable in Germany or be a comparable body in the EU or EEA. You need a donation receipt; up to 300 euros the bank statement is enough. Money sent to a charity or place of worship outside the EU usually does not count.

Ausbildungskosten goes in Anlage N, Anlage Sonderausgaben

Are you studying or training, or did you finish in one of the last years?

What it does to your tax
Costs of a second qualification, such as a master's degree after a bachelor's, count in full as work costs and can be carried into later years. Costs of a first degree count up to 6,000 euros a year, but only in the year you paid them.
The rule
Fees, books, a laptop, travel to the university and a semester abroad all count. If the costs of a second qualification were higher than your income, a return for that year records a loss that lowers your tax in your first working years. A bachelor's degree or completed vocational training from abroad counts as a first qualification too.

Werkstudenten und Minijob goes in Anlage N

Did you work as a student: a working-student job, an internship or a mini-job?

What it does to your tax
Wage tax withheld from a student job usually comes back in full, because the income of the year stays under the basic allowance of 12,096 euros plus the flat amounts.
The rule
A mini-job that the employer taxes at a flat rate stays out of the return. Every other job appears on a wage tax certificate, and the return sets the tax against your low yearly income. You can file for the last four years.

Health and care

Krankheitskosten goes in Anlage Außergewöhnliche Belastungen

Did you pay medical costs yourself: glasses, dental work, prescribed medicine, therapy, hospital, travel to treatment?

What it does to your tax
The part above your personal threshold lowers your taxable income. The threshold is between 1 and 7 percent of your income; it is lower with children and with a lower income.
The rule
Only costs that no insurer reimbursed count, and the treatment must be medically necessary, with a prescription where one is usual. Where you can, collect such costs in one year, because the threshold applies anew every year.

Behinderten-Pauschbetrag goes in Anlage Außergewöhnliche Belastungen

Do you, your spouse or your child have a recognised disability?

What it does to your tax
A flat amount between 384 and 2,840 euros a year depending on the degree, and 7,400 euros for people who are blind or need constant help. No receipts are needed.
The rule
The degree must be established by the German authorities, starting at a degree of 20. A child's amount can be transferred to the parents. Travel costs get a flat amount of their own from a degree of 80, or 70 with a walking impairment.

Bestattungskosten goes in Anlage Außergewöhnliche Belastungen

Did you pay for the funeral of a relative?

What it does to your tax
What you paid beyond the estate and any insurance counts like medical costs, above your personal threshold.
The rule
Only the funeral itself counts, up to a reasonable amount of about 7,500 euros: undertaker, grave, fees. Mourning clothes, the meal afterwards and travel do not.

Your home

Handwerkerleistungen goes in Anlage Haushaltsnahe Aufwendungen

Did you pay tradespeople for work in your home: repairs, painting, fitting a kitchen, boiler maintenance, the chimney sweep?

What it does to your tax
20 percent of the labour cost comes straight off your tax, up to 1,200 euros a year.
The rule
It applies to the home you live in, rented or owned, in the EU or EEA. Only labour, travel and machine costs count, not materials, so the invoice must show them separately, and you must pay by bank transfer.

Haushaltsnahe Dienstleistungen goes in Anlage Haushaltsnahe Aufwendungen

Do you rent, or do you pay for cleaning, gardening, winter service, care at home or a removal firm?

What it does to your tax
20 percent of the cost comes straight off your tax, up to 4,000 euros a year.
The rule
As a tenant, look at your yearly service charge statement: caretaker, staircase cleaning, garden care, winter service and lift maintenance are in it, and your landlord can certify your share. Services you order yourself need an invoice and a bank transfer. A removal firm for a private move counts here too.

Minijob im Haushalt goes in Anlage Haushaltsnahe Aufwendungen

Do you employ someone in your household as a registered mini-jobber?

What it does to your tax
20 percent of what you pay, up to 510 euros a year, comes straight off your tax.
The rule
The helper must be registered with the Minijob-Zentrale through the household cheque procedure. Its yearly statement is your proof.

Savings and investments

Kapitalerträge goes in Anlage KAP

Do you invest through a broker or bank outside Germany, such as Interactive Brokers or one in your home country?

What it does to your tax
Gains, dividends and interest are taxed at 25 percent plus solidarity surcharge, after your saver's allowance. Losses reduce later gains.
The rule
A foreign broker withholds no German tax, so everything has to be declared, converted into euros at the rate of each day. Foreign tax withheld on dividends is credited up to the rate in the tax treaty. Losses on shares can only be set against gains on shares, and a loss carried forward has to be declared every year.

Sparer-Pauschbetrag goes in Anlage KAP

Did you earn interest or dividends at German banks, and did you give them an exemption order?

What it does to your tax
The first 1,000 euros a year are tax-free, 2,000 euros for couples. Tax withheld on them comes back through the return.
The rule
Banks only leave the allowance untaxed if you gave them an exemption order (Freistellungsauftrag). If you did not, or spread it badly across banks, declare the income and the tax office refunds the difference. If your personal tax rate is below 25 percent, you can ask for the lower rate on all your investment income.

Ausländische Kapitalerträge goes in Anlage KAP

Do you have savings, deposits or investment accounts in another country?

What it does to your tax
Interest and gains from them are taxable in Germany from the day you live here, less the allowance and any foreign tax that can be credited.
The rule
As a resident you are taxed on your investment income worldwide. Banks in most countries report the accounts of German residents to the German tax office automatically. Income earned before you moved here is not taxed.

Private Veräußerungsgeschäfte goes in Anlage SO

Did you sell or exchange crypto assets?

What it does to your tax
Gains are tax-free when you held the coins for more than a year. Otherwise they are taxed at your personal rate, unless all such gains of the year stay under 1,000 euros.
The rule
Swapping one coin for another counts as a sale. The limit was 600 euros until 2023. It is all or nothing: at 1,000 euros or more, the whole gain is taxed. Losses can be set against such gains of other years if you declare them.

Mitarbeiteraktien goes in Anlage N, Anlage KAP

Did you receive shares, RSUs or stock options from your employer?

What it does to your tax
The value at the moment you receive the shares is taxed as salary. A later sale is taxed only on the gain since then.
The rule
The salary part is normally already in your wage tax certificate. Keep the vesting statements: the price at vesting is your purchase price for the sale. If the shares sit with a broker abroad, the sale belongs in your return like any foreign investment income.

The return itself

Pflichtveranlagung goes in Hauptvordruck

Are you obliged to file, or is it up to you?

What it does to your tax
If filing is voluntary, you can look at the result first and only file when money comes back.
The rule
Filing is mandatory, among other cases, with income abroad or wage replacement above 410 euros, with tax classes III and V or IV with a factor, with two jobs at once, or with other income above 410 euros. If you file yourself, the deadline is 31 July of the following year.

Antragsveranlagung goes in Hauptvordruck

Have you filed for every year since you arrived?

What it does to your tax
Each year you have not filed may still hold a refund. A voluntary return can be handed in for four years.
The rule
The return for 2022 is possible until 31 December 2026, the one for 2023 until the end of 2027, and so on. If a voluntary return ends in a payment, you can withdraw it within a month of the assessment.

Steuerbescheid goes in Mein ELSTER

Did you check the assessment the tax office sent after your last return?

What it does to your tax
Mistakes, and items the tax office left out, can be corrected, but only for a short time.
The rule
You have one month from receipt to object, in writing or through Mein ELSTER. At its end, under the explanations, the assessment lists every point where the tax office departed from your return.

Bankverbindung goes in Hauptvordruck

Does the tax office have a bank account for your refund?

What it does to your tax
Without one the refund is not paid out.
The rule
Enter the IBAN of an account in the EU or EEA in the main form. It may be someone else's account if you say so there.
